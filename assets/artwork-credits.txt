DEHRADUN LANDMARK WATERCOLORS

These coloured architectural sketches were created with OpenAI ImageGen
using the following photographs as architectural references. The photos
were transformed into isolated watercolor artwork; the sketches are
illustrations, not photographs or images of the wedding venue.
